## Reflection Questions

1. **Did your estimations reflect the actual effort?**
   The estimates should be compared after the sprint because completed story points
   reveal whether the initial complexity assessment was realistic.

2. **Was your backlog well-prioritized?**
   Items with the highest user and business impact should be placed at the top,
   followed by lower-risk supporting features.

3. **How did your simulated sprint align with your plan?**
   The sprint board and burndown chart show the simulated transition from To Do
   through In Progress to Done.

4. **What insights did the burndown chart give about your team's capacity?**
   A chart close to the guideline indicates predictable delivery; sharp drops or
   flat sections indicate work completed in batches or blocked work.
